How to Set Up and Use a POS System for Beginners

Setting up a POS system is usually simple:

  1. Create your business account – Enter your business details and set up users.
  2. Add your products – Enter product names, prices, buying costs, and opening stock.
  3. Set up inventory – Organize products into categories and add suppliers or locations if needed.
  4. Start recording sales – Select products, enter quantities, choose the payment method, and complete the sale.
  5. Monitor your business – Use reports to track sales, stock levels, expenses, and profits.
  6. Perform regular stock takes – Compare physical stock with the quantities recorded in the system.

Once these basics are set up, a POS system can handle much of the daily record-keeping automatically, making it easier to know what you sold, what you have in stock, and how much your business is making.

Does the POS system include inventory management?
Yes. Ruo System includes inventory management, allowing you to track products, monitor stock levels and identify items that need to be reordered.
Can I access my business information from anywhere?
Yes. Ruo System lets you stay connected to your business and monitor sales, inventory and reports without being physically present at your shop.
