COMMON QUESTIONS

Hardware shop POS frequently asked questions.

What is a POS system for a hardware shop?

A POS system helps a hardware business record sales, manage product quantities and review business reports. Ruo System brings these operations together in one platform.

Can I manage different hardware products using Ruo System?

Yes. You can add products such as cement, paints, plumbing fittings, electrical supplies, tools and other items sold in your hardware shop.

Can I use a barcode scanner with Ruo System?

Yes. Ruo System supports compatible barcode scanners to help identify products during checkout.

Can I monitor sales made by my employees?

Ruo System supports staff accounts and activity monitoring, helping you review transactions recorded by your team.

Can I track cash and M-Pesa sales?

Yes. You can review cash and M-Pesa sales records through the business dashboard.

How much does a hardware shop POS system cost in Kenya?

Ruo System subscriptions start at KSh 100 per day. The monthly plan costs KSh 2,000, while the yearly plan costs KSh 20,000.

Can I try Ruo System before subscribing?

Yes. You can explore the demo to see how the system works before choosing a subscription.
