Frequently Asked Questions

What to know when choosing a POS system for your bookshop.

Can a POS system manage books and stationery?

Yes. A general retail POS can manage products such as textbooks, exercise books, pens, files, and other stationery, provided they are recorded as products in the system.

Can I track bookshop sales every day?

A POS system records sales transactions and can provide sales reports, helping you review your business activity over time.

Can I monitor profit from bookshop sales?

Recording buying and selling prices can help you review estimated profit on sales. Remember that operating expenses and other costs also affect your final net profit.
