Why Do Some POS Systems Charge an Installation Fee?

Traditional POS setups can involve more than just the software. A provider may need to install and configure the system, connect hardware, add products, set up printers, configure users and train staff.

When a provider sends someone to your business to do this work, the cost of that service may appear as a POS installation fee.

Do You Actually Need POS Installation?

Not necessarily.

Cloud-based POS systems can work differently. Instead of installing software on your computer, the system is hosted online and you access it through the internet.

That means you can avoid the traditional process of having someone come to your shop and install the software.

What Should You Ask a POS Provider?

Before choosing a POS system, don't only ask about the monthly price. Ask whether there are additional setup costs.

  • Is there an installation fee?
  • Is product setup included?
  • Is staff training included?
  • Is hardware included?
  • Are there additional user or branch charges?
  • Are integrations charged separately?

A low monthly price can look attractive, but you should always understand the total cost of getting started.
