Our process

How our stocktaking service works

We plan the exercise with you before counting begins. This helps set clear expectations, organise the team and minimise disruption to normal business.

01

Initial site visit and assessment

One of our personnel visits your business to assess the stock, shop layout, storage areas, product range, available records and the requirements for the exercise.

02

Agree on the stocktaking plan

We agree on the date, areas to be counted, estimated duration, team size, responsibilities, reporting requirements and quotation before the work is scheduled.

03

Preparation one day before

We coordinate with your team to organise shelves and storage, prepare the latest available stock records, identify unlabelled items and separate damaged or returned goods where practical.

04

Team briefing and task allocation

Before counting starts, our team meets with your designated staff, confirms the method and assigns counting areas to reduce missed products and duplicate counts.

05

Physical stock count

The team counts products in the agreed areas and records quantities. Depending on the stock and available equipment, counting may be manual, barcode-assisted or a combination of methods.

06

Verification and recounts

We review unclear entries and investigate questionable counts. Items with mismatched descriptions, units or quantities may be recounted before the results are finalised.

07

Reconciliation and reporting

We compare the verified physical count with the records provided and prepare a report showing matching quantities, variances and items requiring further investigation.

08

Review and approved adjustments

We review the findings with you. Where included in the agreed scope, verified quantities can be used to update your inventory records after approval by an authorised person.

To support an accurate count: we agree in advance how sales, deliveries, returns and other stock movements will be handled while counting is underway. Differences are reported for investigation; they are not assumed to be theft or automatically treated as losses.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How often should a retail shop conduct stocktaking?

It depends on your business. Some shops conduct a full stocktake quarterly or annually, while others count selected high-value or fast-moving products more frequently.

Can you count stock if we do not use Ruo System?

Yes. We can discuss your current stock records and POS or inventory system during the initial assessment and agree on a suitable approach.

Will stocktaking interrupt our normal business?

We plan the exercise with you in advance. Depending on the shop and stock volume, we may recommend counting outside busy hours or recording stock movements separately while the count is underway.

What happens if the physical count differs from the POS?

The difference is recorded for review. It may be caused by unrecorded sales, receiving errors, returns, damage, incorrect units or other issues. We report the discrepancy so you can investigate and approve any necessary correction.

Will you update our inventory after counting?

Inventory updates can be included in the agreed scope. Any adjustments should be reviewed and approved by you or an authorised representative before records are changed.

What information should we prepare before the assessment?

If available, prepare your current stock list, product codes or barcodes, details of storage areas, and information about how sales, deliveries and returns are recorded. We will confirm any additional requirements during the site visit.
