Questions about cosmetic shop stocktaking

Who is this stocktaking service for?

This service is intended for cosmetic shop owners who need a physical count of their products, help reviewing stock quantities or clearer inventory records.

Do I need to have a POS system already?

You will need to have a POS system and if you dont our POS system would work best for it is made specifically for Cosmetic shops

Will stocktaking fix all my stock discrepancies?

A physical count can help identify differences between counted quantities and available records. The cause of each discrepancy may need further review, and the count alone does not prevent future differences.
