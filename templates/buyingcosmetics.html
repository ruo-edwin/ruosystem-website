If you're looking for where to buy beauty products in bulk in Nairobi, there are plenty of suppliers around Nairobi CBD. But if you want to save yourself time, these are three places worth checking depending on what you're looking for.

Quick answer: Visit Vision Cosmetics for braids, weaves and crochets, Sunrays Cosmetics for wet hair and body products, and Excel Complex for makeup, skincare and beauty accessories.

1. Vision Cosmetics – Braids, Weaves & Crochets

Vision Cosmetics, located along Dubois Road in Excel Complex Building, is a good place to start if you're mainly looking for hair products.

They specialise particularly in braids, weaves and crochets from major brands.

What you can find

  • Braids from major companies including Darling, Angels, Lush and Sistar
  • Weaves and crochets
  • Hair extensions
  • Hair treatments
  • Shampoos and conditioners
  • Styling gels and edge control
  • Hair dyes

One useful thing about buying from an experienced distributor is that you don't necessarily have to know exactly what to stock when you're starting out.

If you're a new customer, you can explain your budget and they can help you identify fast-moving products to start with.

Vision Cosmetics: 0713 995 664

2. Sunrays Cosmetics – Wet Hair & Body Products

If you're specifically looking for wet hair products, Sunrays Cosmetics is another place worth checking.

Sunrays is located along Dubois Road near the Kensilver bus station area and stocks a wide range of products for hair and body care.

What you can find

  • Shampoos and conditioners
  • Hair treatments
  • Body lotions and roll-ons
  • Styling gels and edge control
  • Hair dyes
  • Relaxers

In simple terms, if you're looking for products that customers regularly use on their hair and body, this is one of the places you can check when restocking.

3. Makeup, Skincare & Accessories – Excel Complex

For makeup, skincare and beauty accessories, Excel Complex Building in Nairobi CBD is a useful place to start because you can find several vendors selling different products under one roof.

Depending on your customers, you can look for:

  • Foundations
  • Concealers
  • Powders
  • Lipsticks and lip gloss
  • Eyelashes
  • Makeup brushes and sponges
  • Body lotions
  • Face-care products

If you're starting a beauty business, it's usually better to test smaller quantities first. Once you know what your customers prefer, you can increase the quantities you buy.
