How Much Does a POS System Cost in Kenya?

Published by Ruo System

One of the first questions business owners ask when looking for a Point of Sale (POS) system is, "How much does a POS system cost in Kenya?" By the time you're asking this question, you've likely already realized that your business needs a POS system. Perhaps you're struggling to track stock, monitor sales, manage multiple cashiers, or generate accurate business reports. The next step is finding a reliable POS system that fits both your business needs and your budget.

The good news is that POS systems in Kenya have become significantly more affordable over the years. Today, businesses of all sizes—from small retail shops and boutiques to supermarkets, pharmacies, and hardware stores— can access modern POS technology without making a huge upfront investment. However, understanding the difference between traditional POS systems and modern cloud-based POS systems is important before making a decision.

Traditional POS Systems: High Upfront Costs

Traditionally, POS systems were installed directly on a business computer and could only be accessed from that computer or from other computers connected through a local network. In most cases, one computer acted as the main server while other devices connected to it within the business premises.

While these systems were effective at the time, they came with several limitations. Accessing business data remotely was often impossible, software updates required technical support, and businesses had to invest heavily in hardware infrastructure.

Traditionally, a complete POS installation in Kenya could cost KSh 50,000 to KSh 200,000 or more, depending on the hardware, software, installation, licensing and support required. Modern cloud-based POS systems have changed this pricing model, with many providers charging a monthly subscription instead of requiring a large upfront investment.

For many small and medium-sized businesses in Kenya, these costs were simply too high. As a result, POS systems were often viewed as tools reserved for large supermarkets, wholesalers, and established enterprises with significant budgets.

Modern Cloud-Based POS Systems: Affordable and Accessible

The rise of cloud technology has completely transformed the POS industry. Today, most modern POS systems are web-based, meaning business owners can access their systems through a website, mobile application, tablet, or computer from virtually anywhere with an internet connection.

Instead of storing all information on a single computer within the business premises, cloud POS systems securely store data online. This allows business owners to monitor sales, track inventory, and view reports whether they are at the shop, at home, or even traveling.

One of the biggest advantages of modern POS systems is affordability. Unlike traditional systems that require a large upfront payment, cloud-based POS systems typically operate on a subscription model.

Why Subscription-Based POS Systems Are Growing in Kenya

Instead of spending tens or hundreds of thousands of shillings upfront, businesses can start using a professional POS system immediately while paying a manageable monthly fee.

  • Access your business from anywhere
  • Automatic cloud backups
  • Automatic software updates
  • Real-time inventory tracking
  • Scalable as your business grows
  • Lower startup costs

So, What Is the Average Cost of a POS System in Kenya?

Traditional POS Systems

  • Installation Fee: Often required
  • Setup Cost: KSh 50,000 – KSh 200,000+
  • Maintenance Costs: Additional charges may apply
  • Upgrades: Often charged separately

Modern Cloud-Based POS Systems

  • Low setup costs
  • Monthly subscription model
  • Cloud backups included
  • Ongoing support and updates

Many cloud-based POS systems in Kenya charge between KSh 1,000 and KSh 5,000 per month, depending on the features offered.
