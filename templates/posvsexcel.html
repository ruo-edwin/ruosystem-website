POS vs Excel: Which Is Better for a Small Business in Kenya?

Should your business use Excel or a POS system to manage sales, stock and profit? Here's the simple difference.

If you're running a small business in Kenya, you may be wondering whether you should continue using Excel or switch to a POS system. The simple answer is: Excel requires you to manage the data. A POS system is designed to manage the process for you.

Excel is useful for recording and analysing business data. But as your sales and stock increase, manually updating spreadsheets can become time-consuming and prone to errors.

A POS system is built specifically around the daily process of selling products, managing stock and understanding your business numbers.

02 — EXCEL

When Is Excel Enough?

Excel can work perfectly well for a very small business. If you only have a few products and a small number of daily transactions, there may be no reason to complicate things.

Excel can be enough if you have:

  • Few products
  • Few daily sales
  • No employees making sales
  • Simple stock requirements
  • Someone available to maintain the spreadsheet

Excel is not a bad business tool. The problem starts when you are using a spreadsheet to manually manage a process that has become too large.

03 — POS

When Should You Use a POS System?

A POS system becomes more useful as your business starts handling more products, customers and transactions.

You may need a POS if:

  • Your stock is becoming difficult to track.
  • You have employees making sales.
  • You want automatic sales reports.
  • You need to know your profit.
  • Your physical stock doesn't match your records.
  • You are spending too much time updating Excel.

Instead of separately recording a sale, calculating the amount, updating stock and preparing reports, a POS can connect these processes together.

Sell a product → record the sale → reduce stock → update your reports.

04 — THE BOTTOM LINE

POS vs Excel: Which One Should You Choose?

Excel is a great tool for recording and analysing data.

A POS system is built to manage the sales and stock process.

If your business is very small and you only make a few transactions every day, Excel may be perfectly adequate.

But if your business is growing and you're spending more time maintaining spreadsheets than managing the business, it may be time to move to a POS system.

The goal isn't to replace Excel just because you can. The goal is to use the right tool for the stage your business is at.
